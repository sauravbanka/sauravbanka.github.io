I'm a master's student in Computer Science at **ETH Zürich**, working at the intersection of machine learning and systems. I'm interested in theoretically grounded algorithms and practical infrastructure that make large-scale training and inference more efficient.

I am a research assistant in the **Efficient Architecture and Systems Lab (EASL)** at ETH Zürich, supervised by Prof. Ana Klimovic, and a research fellow in the **Scalable Computing Systems Laboratory (SaCS)** at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Anne-Marie Kermarrec.

Previously, I worked on distributed systems for **AWS Lambda** and was a research science intern at **IBM Research Zürich**. I studied Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at **UC Berkeley**, where I also worked on machine learning for gravitational lens discovery at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.

## Research interests

- **Agentic RL infrastructure:** sandboxing and scheduling for efficient, long-horizon training.
- **Efficient ML systems:** MoE inference, co-located training, and better use of heterogeneous compute.
- **Systems algorithms and abstractions:** learned policies, resource allocation, and hardware-agnostic measures of computational work.
